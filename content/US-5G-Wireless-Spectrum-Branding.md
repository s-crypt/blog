I needed a reference of what spectrum is used by which carrier, and what band it falls into. I made this chart so I might as well put it out there if anybody else is looking for this information, but I am probably not going to update it.

Low-Band is 600 MHz -> 900 MHz
Mid-Band is 1 GHz -> 6 GHz
High-Band is 24 GHz -> 47 GHz
